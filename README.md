# 🚀 Career Management System

A comprehensive web-based platform designed to support students in planning and managing their academic and career paths efficiently. This system aims to offer **personalized guidance**, **centralized data management**, and **actionable recommendations** to help students make informed decisions about jobs, higher education, and skill development.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Career Management System** is built to bridge the gap between student potential and career opportunities by aligning their academic performance, skills, and preferences with relevant prospects. It provides a **centralized dashboard** for academic records, extracurricular achievements, skill assessments, and future goals.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Help students understand their academic standing and areas of improvement
* Recommend job roles, internships, higher education, or certifications based on student profiles
* Act as a mentor by tracking student progress and offering career guidance
* Store historical academic data and suggest personalized career paths

---

## 🧠 Key Features

* 📂 **Academic Tracker** — Upload and monitor semester-wise GPA/CGPA and subject-wise grades
* 🎓 **Higher Education Planner** — Recommendations based on skills and academic scores
* 💼 **Job Preparation Guide** — Resume building, project linking, and job role mapping
* 📊 **Skill Mapping** — Compare your current skills with job role requirements
* 📁 **Profile Dashboard** — Centralized view of education, experience, and goals
* 👨‍🏫 **Mentor Panel** — Professors can track mentee growth, offer feedback, and give suggestions

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **Frontend**: React, HTML, CSS, JavaScript (via Vite)
* **Backend**: Python (Flask or Django, based on actual usage)
* **Database**: MySQL / SQLite
* **Version Control**: Git & GitHub

---

## 📁 Modules

### 1. 👩‍🎓 Student Dashboard

* View academic details (GPA, CGPA)
* Upload and manage project links

### 2. 👨‍🏫 Mentor Panel

* View mentee records
* Add remarks and monitor progress
* Suggest growth paths

### 3. 💡 Career Recommendation

* Suggest jobs, higher studies, and skills
* Based on academic history and interests

### 4. 📄 Resume & Projects

* Add resume links
* Showcase mini and major projects

### 5. 🏅 Scholarships & Achievements

* Track scholarships received
* Record extracurricular accomplishments
